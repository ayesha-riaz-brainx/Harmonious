Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Harmonious collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Harmonious mobile app and related services (together, the “Service”).

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide while using the Service, including:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address;
  • Profile, wellness, health, and goal information you enter;
  • Journal entries, daily logs, check-ins, and other content you create;
  • Messages and inputs you provide to the AI coaching and capture features; and
  • Settings and preferences, including communication, memory, and privacy preferences.

We may also process technical information needed to operate the Service, such as authentication and session information, device and network information, and error or security information.

How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain the Service, create and secure your account, personalize app features, respond to requests, prevent misuse, and improve reliability. Information you provide to AI-powered features is used to generate the requested response or feature result.

Health and wellness information

Harmonious provides general wellness and self-reflection features. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Do not use the Service for emergencies or as a substitute for professional healthcare.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may use service providers that help us operate the Service, including providers for authentication, cloud data storage, email delivery, and AI functionality. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users, the Service, or the public.

Your choices and controls

You can review and update information in the app. The app also provides controls for AI memory and privacy preferences. You can request an export of your account data from the “Privacy & data export” section in the app. You may delete your account from the app; deleting an account removes the account and associated data held by the Service, subject to any retention required by law or necessary for security and fraud prevention.

Data retention and security

We retain information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.
